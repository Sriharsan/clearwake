## What to say, and what to avoid

1. Say "Seller Protection on agentic transactions covers item not received claims." Do not say the buyer is covered.
2. Say "nearly two thirds of 498 US merchants." Never print a number like 64 percent.
3. Quote Worldpay without a month.
4. Say the Standard fee is waived for inquiries not escalated to a claim. Do not claim the High Volume fee is waived.
